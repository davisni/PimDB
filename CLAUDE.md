# PimDB

Design and prototype of a Product Information Management database for
Reliable Aftermarket Parts (RAP).

- `docs/design.md` is the current design. Read it first.
- `docs/decisions.md` is the decision log. Append to it when something is decided.
- `docs/open-questions.md` tracks what is undecided.
- `docs/rap-pim-design.html` is the source of the published visual version
  (https://claude.ai/artifact/JiNYjnqXUtEGYEcoQ6xYTf). Publish updates to that
  same artifact rather than creating a new one.

Use the vocabulary in `docs/design.md` exactly: item, branded product, listing,
offer, fact.

Explanations should be plain-language with pictures and worked examples.
Example part numbers, prices and IDs must be marked as invented.

NetSuite work is against the sandbox only, and read-only unless asked otherwise.
